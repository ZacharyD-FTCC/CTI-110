# How to Make a Robot Sandwich

## Assumptions

- You have two slices of bread.
- You have sandwich filling.
- You have a plate.
- The robot can follow instructions.

## Steps

1. Place one slice of bread on the plate.
2. Place the sandwich filling on the bread.
3. Place the second slice of bread on top.
4. Press down gently on the sandwich.
5. Pick up the sandwich.

**Done When:** The sandwich is assembled with the filling between the two slices of bread.
