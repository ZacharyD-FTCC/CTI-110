# How to Make a Robot Sandwich

## Assumptions

- You have two slices of bread.
- You have your sandwich filling.
- You have a plate.
- The robot can follow simple instructions.
- The robot does not know what a sandwich is unless it is explained.

## Steps

1. Place one slice of bread on the plate.
2. Pick up the sandwich filling.
3. Place the sandwich filling in the center of the bread.
4. Pick up the second slice of bread.
5. Place the second slice of bread on top of the filling.
6. Press down gently on the top slice of bread.
7. Pick up the completed sandwich.

**Done When:** The sandwich is assembled with the filling between the two slices of bread.
